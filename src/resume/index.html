Summary

A pre-med student doesn't usually end up naming the credit card in your wallet. I studied pre-med at Cornell and apprenticed under the poet A.R. Ammons. Then I spent twenty years writing the line, directing the frame, and building the campaign. Two arcs. Branding: Brand Institute, Siegel & Gale, FutureBrand, Interbrand. Advertising: McGarryBowen, BBH, Publicis, Ogilvy. For the last two years I have folded AI into that craft: Claude, Veo, Runway, Midjourney. The craft came first.
