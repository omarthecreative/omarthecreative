Who is Omar Silwany?

Omar Y. Silwany is a Global Creative Director and Writer with 20+ years across the most rigorous branding and advertising shops in the industry. He holds a BA in Literature and Creative Writing from Cornell University, where he was mentored by Pulitzer-nominated poet A.R. Ammons. He runs Meanwhile In Jupiter LLC, an AI filmmaking and branded content consultancy, from Palm Coast, Florida. He is reachable at info@omarsilwany.com or (917) 513-0396, and on LinkedIn at https://www.linkedin.com/in/omarsilwany.

What is his positioning?

Pre-med precision meets poetic instinct. Madison Avenue craft applied to AI technology. He begins every engagement from brand strategy and executes through film. He is not an "AI creative director." He is a Creative Director who integrates AI as a production layer beneath human craft. Final hero films are produced with real directors, real crews, and real lenses; AI is used for concepting, previs, and production augmentation.

What is his experience?

Staff creative roles at McGarryBowen Dentsu (2006–2009), Momentum Worldwide / McCann (2012–2015), and BBH USA (2020–2021). Strategic branding lineage at Brand Institute, Siegel & Gale, The Coleman Group, FutureBrand, and Interbrand from 1997 through 2003. Independent practice through Professor LLC (2009–2012 and 2015–2020) and now Meanwhile In Jupiter LLC (2020–present). Freelance and consulting partnerships with Ogilvy, Publicis Groupe, BBH USA, Fundamentalco, JOAN, The Community, VaynerX, Matter Unlimited, Erich & Kallman, CP+B, Seven Bucks, SET Creative, Red Interactive, GhostRobot, Firstborn, GROW, Momentum, Translation, Deutsch, Dentsu, and Grey.

What is his key work?

Named Lipitor and Nexium, two of the world's best-selling pharmaceuticals. Architected the Chase Sapphire verbal identity at McGarryBowen Dentsu. Conceived the 64 Days of Crayola at McGarryBowen Dentsu, the most effective retail campaign in Crayola's history. Named and developed the tone of voice for Google VR Window Wonderland, an immersive experience created by GROW — awarded Webby, FWA, Awwwards. Contributed to $10M in Google account growth at BBH USA (contract role). 50M+ social impressions on My Brother's Keeper Alliance through Matter Unlimited (Obama Foundation, Steph Curry, Chance the Rapper). 100M+ Instagram views on Project Rock through Seven Bucks Creative, every product sold out. Press across projects: The New York Times, The Washington Post, Financial Times, The Guardian, AdWeek, Fast Company, PSFK, USA Today, People, Esquire, Campaign US, Mashable, Associated Press.

What does he offer?

Freelance and fractional Creative Direction. Staff Creative Director or Group Creative Director roles. Project-based engagements through Meanwhile In Jupiter LLC, including concept sprints, pitch leadership, brand voice and naming systems, manifesto writing, and brand film direction with AI-augmented production. Disciplines span creative direction, copywriting, brand naming, verbal identity, scriptwriting, storyboarding, integrated campaigns, experiential marketing, broadcast, digital content, brand films, and AI filmmaking.

What is his AI capability?

He operates a working AI filmmaking practice, not a theoretical one. Current stack includes Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini, Veo, Sora, Runway, DALL-E, Midjourney, ElevenLabs, and Adobe Firefly. He uses AI for concepting, previs, and production augmentation while preserving human-led final film craft. He has built reusable agentic systems for narrative engineering, world-building, and emotional performance direction.

Where is he located, and is he open to relocation?

Palm Coast, Florida. Remote-friendly. Open to relocate for the right role. Comfortable with hybrid and on-site engagements when the project requires it.

What does it cost?

Project-based pricing. Day rates and weekly rates available for freelance engagements. Retainer arrangements available for fractional Creative Director engagements. Typical response time to qualified inquiries is under 24 hours.

How does it work?

Brief to concept to execution. Engagements typically begin with a short conversation by email or LinkedIn DM. From there: strategic framing, concept development, copy and visual direction, then production. For brand film work, AI is integrated where it accelerates exploration and previs; final hero films are produced with real directors, real crews, and real lenses.

Brand CMOs and brand marketing leaders who need a senior creative partner who can move from strategy through execution. Agency Creative Directors and ECDs who need freelance senior creative firepower or pitch leadership. Recruiters and talent partners staffing CD, GCD, ECD, or fractional CCO roles. Founders building category-defining brands who need the seam between human craft and AI production held by one operator.
